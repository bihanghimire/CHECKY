OPENINGS

A chess opening is the first part of a chess game, where both players develop their pieces and fight for control of the center.
The main goals of an opening are:
* Control the center — especially the squares e4, d4, e5, and d5.,
*Develop your pieces — bring your knights and bishops into useful positions.
* Protect your king — usually by castling.
* Develop quickly — avoid moving the same piece repeatedly without a good reason.
in this website openings will be classified into two major types . that are openings played with white and those played with black
