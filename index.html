♟ WELCOME TO CHECKY

Welcome to CHECKY — a chess guide made for people who are just a little too obsessed with chess.

This website focuses on opening theory, endgame theory, and improving your tactical vision.

📖 OPENINGS

Learn the ideas, plans, and variations behind different chess openings.

The goal isn't to memorize 25 moves of theory just because some grandmaster played it once. Instead, we'll focus on understanding why the moves are played.

Hopefully, you'll also stop getting completely destroyed in the first 10 moves. 😭

♜ ENDGAMES

Endgames are where all that hard work is supposed to pay off.

We'll cover important endgame positions, techniques, patterns, and ideas that can help you turn an advantage into an actual win.

Because having an extra queen is pretty useless if you somehow manage to stalemate your opponent. 💀
