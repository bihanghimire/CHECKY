How to Face the Scotch as Black

The Scotch begins after: 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4.
Black's main responses include 3...exd4 4.Nxd4 Bc5, 4...Nf6, and 4...Qh4.

Conclusion

The Scotch Game is an open chess opening beginning with 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4.

White immediately challenges the center and usually obtains active piece development and tactical opportunities.

Black can choose several important setups, including the Classical, Schmidt, and Steinitz Variations.
