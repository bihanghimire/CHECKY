♟ CHECKY — Your Guide to Chess

Welcome to CHECKY, a chess learning website for people who are just a little too obsessed with chess. ♟️

Whether you're looking to understand opening theory, master endgames, or improve your tactical vision, CHECKY is here to help you become a better chess player — one move at a time.

The goal isn't to memorize endless variations. It's to understand why moves work, recognize patterns, and make smarter decisions over the board.

Because let's be honest, losing your queen on move 8 isn't exactly part of the master plan. 😭

🎯 Our Goal

CHECKY aims to make chess learning more approachable, engaging, and understandable.

Instead of simply memorizing moves, you'll focus on understanding positions, recognizing patterns, and learning the reasoning behind good decisions.

Whether you're a casual player or an aspiring chess enthusiast, there's always something new to discover.
